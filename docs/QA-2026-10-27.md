# Dashboard audit and fixes — 27 October 2026

Local audit and remediation using Chrome, an isolated temporary SQLite database and synthetic clients. Production deployment and live integrations are outside the exercised environment. Existing production records were not edited and no real messages, payments or Calendar events were sent.

## Findings

Root causes below are CONFIRMED by source inspection, browser reproduction, or the recorded measurements. Each change has an automated browser regression scenario. The initial layout sweep recorded Clients overflowing by 34px at 1440px, 362px at 820px, 792px at 390px and 862px at 320px. A separate 1280×600 check found Team below the viewport (bottom 638px) with 737px of unscrollable sidebar content.

- `qa/artifacts/audit-ledger.json`: viewport/route measurements and captured page errors.

- `qa/dashboard-edge-cases.mjs`: regressions for findings in this pass.
